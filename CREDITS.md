# Credits

Graphite includes compact public-domain media derivatives in its user-testable sandbox vault. Attribution is not required for these works, but it helps people discover the collections that preserved and shared them.

## Le'Agréable

`sandbox-vault/Attachments/le-agreable.jpg` is a resized JPEG derivative of *Le'Agréable* by François-Nicolas Martinet, from *Histoire naturelle des dorades de la Chine* (1780).

- Source: [Public Domain Image Archive](https://pdimagearchive.org/images/0d585ef1-1f27-4a0b-a9b1-7d308a863649/)
- Holding sources identified by the archive: Internet Archive and Harvard University
- Rights: Public Domain Worldwide; no additional digital rights asserted

## A Trip Down Market Street Before the Fire

`sandbox-vault/Attachments/market-street-1906.mp4` is a locally transcoded ten-second, 360p excerpt beginning at 4:10 in *A trip down Market Street before the fire* by the Miles Brothers (1906).

- Source: [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/00694408/)
- Rights: Public domain and free to use and reuse
- Credit line: Library of Congress, Motion Picture, Broadcasting, and Recorded Sound Division, Before and After the Great Earthquake and Fire: Early Films of San Francisco, 1897 to 1916 Collection

## 12th Street Rag

`sandbox-vault/Attachments/12th-street-rag.mp3` is a 30-second Citizen DJ excerpt from the 1920 recording *12th Street rag*.

- Composer: Euday L. Bowman
- Performers: All Star Trio; Victor Arden and Max Kortlander, piano; George Hamilton Green, xylophone; Wheeler Wadsworth, saxophone
- Sources: [Citizen DJ remix](https://citizen-dj.labs.loc.gov/loc-jukebox-jazz/remix/?itemId=jukebox-38191&itemStart=1000) and [Library of Congress item](https://www.loc.gov/item/jukebox-38191/)
- Rights: Public domain under the Music Modernization Act; free to use and reuse
- Suggested credit: Citizen DJ Project, Library of Congress, National Jukebox

## Beyond Earth

`sandbox-vault/Attachments/beyond-earth-excerpt.pdf` is a four-page excerpt from *Beyond Earth: A Chronicle of Deep Space Exploration, 1958-2016* by Asif A. Siddiqi (NASA SP-2018-4041, 2018). It preserves the cover, title page, catalog and publication page, and the opening “Firsts in the History of Deep Space Exploration” page for multipage PDF embed testing.

- Sources: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beyond_Earth_by_Asif_Siddiqi_(2018).pdf) and [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/beyond-earth-a-chronicle-of-deep-space-exploration/)
- Credit: NASA / Asif A. Siddiqi
- Rights: Public domain in the United States because the work was created solely by NASA

The remaining sandbox fixtures were created specifically for Graphite.

## Theme Inspirations

Graphite's bundled palettes and typography are original adaptations. They do not include upstream Obsidian theme CSS, images, or other theme assets. These projects informed the visual direction and deserve a clear path back to their creators:

- **GitHub:** [GitHub Theme](https://github.com/krios2146/obsidian-theme-github) by krios2146, which adapts GitHub's interface colors for Obsidian
- **Things:** [Things](https://github.com/colineckert/obsidian-things) by Colin Eckert, inspired by Cultured Code's Things app
- **Shimmering Focus:** [Shimmering Focus](https://github.com/chrisgrieser/shimmering-focus) by Chris Grieser
- **Cupertino:** [Cupertino](https://github.com/aaaaalexis/obsidian-cupertino) by Alexis
- **Prism:** [Prism](https://github.com/damiankorcz/Prism-Theme) by Damian Korcz
- **Solarized:** [Solarized](https://github.com/altercation/solarized) by Ethan Schoonover and [Solarized for Obsidian](https://github.com/harmtemolder/obsidian-solarized) by Harm Temolder

Default, Nebula, and Void are Graphite-original themes. The upstream projects listed above do not endorse or maintain Graphite.
